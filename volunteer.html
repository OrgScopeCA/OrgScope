Good to know

Questions you probably have.

Can I apply before September?

Yes. You can reach out anytime through our contact page to express interest and share your track and skillset. Applications will be formally reviewed and onboarding will begin once our volunteer cohort opens in mid-September, but reaching out early means you're one of the first considered.

Is this remote?

Yes, entirely. All work happens in a sandbox environment and all communication runs through Org Scope, so you can volunteer from anywhere in the GTA, not just near a specific school.

How much time does this take?

It's task based, not shift based. You claim tasks that fit your schedule, so the time commitment flexes around school, exams, or anything else going on in your life.

Do I need past experience?

No. Some past work helps us match you to the right track faster, but genuine interest and a willingness to learn matter more than a portfolio on day one.
