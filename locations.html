Where we work

Free websites, SEO, and marketing support across Scarborough, North York, and the GTA.

OrgScope's volunteers are based in and around Kennedy & Lawrence in Scarborough, with our reach growing outward across Toronto. Below is where small businesses can currently request a free website, SEO help, flyer, poster, or social media graphics from OrgScope.

Areas we serve

Where OrgScope is currently taking on businesses.

Our volunteers are based right in Scarborough, so it's where we can move fastest, but we're expanding fast across the rest of the GTA.

Kennedy & Lawrence

Kennedy & Lawrence

Businesses in and around Kennedy & Lawrence are described as vibrant, multicultural, and longstanding. OrgScope strives to provide online portfolio help to business owners in areas like Kennedy & Lawrence. It has a magnificent community, with The Taste of Lawrence Festival, known for drawing in around 100,000 people yearly. It speaks a lot about how people show up and care for their local businesses. Many can imagine that quite a few bakeries, restaurants, and mom-and-pop shops along Lawrence Ave rely heavily on such gatherings for word of mouth, rather than a proper website. As with any small business, many would benefit from direct, no-cost help. Getting started is one step away!

Wexford / Wexford Heights

Wexford / Wexford Heights

Wexford Heights is a place where Bengali, Gujarati, Iranian, Nigerian, Somali, and Filipino specialties are on every corner. It is known for its strip-mall plazas, though not the biggest in the world, which bring charm to the community around it. Featuring local bakeries, ethnic restaurants, and service shops. OrgScope understands what it means to be a business owner here and would love to help strengthen your online presence. Speak with us today!

Golden Mile

Golden Mile

Golden Mile is known as Canada's first model industrial park, and in the present day serves as the home to automotive services, car dealerships, and sprawling "big-box" retail plazas. Today, Golden Mile is going through a shift, focusing more on transit development, creating a chance for spaces to be utilized more efficiently. OrgScope is all for it! We are here to give Golden Mile businesses, big or small, help with any aspects of online presence. Fill out the form, and we'll get started!

Markham & Lawrence

Markham & Lawrence

Markham and Lawrence is a tight-knit community, having a focus on commercial areas and is home to the CML Village BIA. With a wide variety of stores, from general to well-known food spots, we are highly confident any business owner in this area would greatly appreciate and benefit from OrgScope's help. What do you have to lose?

Danforth & Victoria Park

Danforth & Victoria Park

The intersection between Danforth & Victoria Park sits right at the edge of Scarborough, described as having almost every corner accessible, a walking heaven, many might say. The north-east corner is home to the well-known Shoppers World Danforth, a total of 326,000 sq. ft. The shopping center serves as a main anchor of the area; smaller businesses and individuals are a reflection of the immediate Oakridge and Crescent Town neighbourhoods, rich and multicultural. Beautiful Venezuelan, Caribbean, and Trinidadian cultures stand out; many eateries, service stores, and mom-and-pop shops would benefit from OrgScope's help for their online portfolio… Why not try it out? Only takes a minute!

Yonge & Finch

Yonge & Finch

Known for its Chinese and Korean eateries, it's a food lovers' heaven. It's a perfect place to get all types of cuisine, especially late at night, where the strip is known for its high energy even after dark! Commercial spaces in Yonge & Finch have small independent retailers, specialty shops, and essential big-box conveniences. We advise businesses here to take advantage of the youth-led initiative OrgScope to help increase their presence in the social space. Apply now at zero cost…

Bathurst & Sheppard

Bathurst & Sheppard

The area Bathurst and Sheppard is in is known as Little Moscow; many of the population there being Russian and Jewish reflects on the type of businesses there as well. Kosher-friendly and traditional bakeries, restaurants, and mom-and-pop shops are well known; OrgScope is here to help small owners who run stores like these with websites, advertisements, and SEO. Are you interested? Get started now.
