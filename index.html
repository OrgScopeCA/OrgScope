Questions

A few things people usually ask.

Is OrgScope actually free?

Yes. OrgScope is a free, student-led initiative. There is no cost to businesses for websites, SEO support, flyers, posters, or social media graphics.

Do I need to give OrgScope access to my accounts?

No. OrgScope never requests account access, logins, or passwords. All work is built independently and delivered to you as a finished product.

What areas does OrgScope serve?

OrgScope primarily supports small businesses across Scarborough, North York, and the wider Greater Toronto Area, with volunteers based in and around the Lawrence and Kennedy area.

How long does a project take?

Timelines vary based on volunteer availability and task complexity, since OrgScope is entirely volunteer-run. There is no guaranteed delivery date, but most requests are reviewed and scoped shortly after submission.
