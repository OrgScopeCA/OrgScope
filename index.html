A student-built initiative

Working websites and marketing in the GTA, without any risk to your business.

OrgScope is a free opportunity for small businesses in the GTA. It is entirely youth-led, with a cohort of vetted volunteers planned to come in mid-September from schools in and around Lawrence and Kennedy. Currently, with a two-step review process, we offer deliverables for websites, SEO support, posters, and social media graphics. Learn more.
